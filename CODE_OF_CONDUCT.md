# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agent Trestle is built to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or affiliation. We commit
to treating participation as a collaboration between people, not a contest.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
- Assume good faith, and ask clarifying questions before assuming malice.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person; accept feedback the same way.
- Respect that maintainers must weigh scope, security, and maintenance cost,
  and may decline a contribution for reasons unrelated to its quality.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct them, and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal, political, or identity-based attacks and insults.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Encouraging or endorsing any of the above.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
code review, commit messages, and discussions — and whenever an individual is
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer
[@trsdn](https://github.com/trsdn). Reports are handled confidentially, and the
reporter's identity is not disclosed without their consent.

Please include what happened, where, when, and any supporting links. You do not
need to be the target of the behaviour to report it.

Security vulnerabilities are **not** handled here — follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will respond to
reports fairly and proportionately. Responses escalate according to severity
and pattern:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation of why the behaviour was
   inappropriate, with a request to stop.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning, with consequences stated for continued
   behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — a time-limited ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from the project and its spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate these expectations, and will explain the
reason when doing so is appropriate.

Enforcement decisions apply to everyone, including maintainers.
